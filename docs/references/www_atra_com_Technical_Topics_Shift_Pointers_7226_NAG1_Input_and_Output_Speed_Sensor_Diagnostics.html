FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about ATRA Membership

What is ATRA?

ATRA is the Automatic Transmission Rebuilders Association, a non-profit trade association serving the transmission and powertrain repair industry since 1954.

What does ATRA do?

ATRA provides technical support, training, certification, industry events, business resources, and consumer support for transmission repair professionals and repair shops.

How do I find a trusted transmission repair shop?

Consumers can use the ATRA Shop Finder to locate trusted transmission repair specialists that follow ATRA’s code of ethics and industry standards.

Does ATRA offer transmission repair training?

Yes. ATRA offers live seminars, online courses, hands-on workshops, certifications, and technical education for transmission repair professionals. Visit our training and events page for more information.

What are the benefits of ATRA membership?

Members receive access to technical hotlines, training resources, diagnostics support, networking opportunities, events, and industry tools designed to help repair shops grow. Visit member benefits page for more information.
